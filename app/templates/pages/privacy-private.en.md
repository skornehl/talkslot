This notice informs you, in accordance with Art. 13 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), which personal data is processed when you use this booking service, for what purpose, and what rights you have.

# 1. Controller

The controller within the meaning of the GDPR for this booking service is:

[[VERANTWORTLICHE]]

This covers everything that happens to your details on this site (booking, changing, cancelling, the data stored for it, abuse protection) and the e-mails the booking service sends, including the summaries to the teacher until they are sent.

The teachers who offer appointments on this platform use this service of the controller.

**The meeting itself** is part of the teacher's work at their school. The same applies to whatever the teacher does with your details outside this booking service, for example: notes on the meeting, the summary e-mails in their mailbox (section 5), calendar entries, printed appointment lists and your replies to e-mails from the booking service (as a rule they go to the teacher). The operator of this booking service is not responsible for that; as a rule the school is. The school's privacy notices and rules apply. Please direct questions about it to the teacher or the school.

**Data protection officer:** none appointed, as there is no legal obligation to do so (Art. 37 GDPR, section 38 BDSG). [PLEASE CHECK, THEN DELETE THIS BRACKET: IF ONE HAS BEEN APPOINTED (E.G. BY AN ASSOCIATION OR INDEPENDENT PROVIDER), GIVE THEIR CONTACT DETAILS INSTEAD OF THE SENTENCE BEFORE.]

# 2. Purpose

Parents and guardians can use this page to book, change and cancel meetings (e.g. parent-teacher conferences) with the teacher named on the booking page. The data is used solely to organise and hold these meetings, including summary e-mails to the teacher, and for no other purpose.

# 3. Data processed

**When you book:**

- the **name(s)** you enter in the text field (required). Please only enter what is needed to identify the meeting (e.g. the child's name) and no other information.
- your **e-mail address**, only if you choose to provide it. It is used solely for the booking confirmation and messages about your appointment (change, cancellation, a reminder the evening before if the teacher has turned it on for the round), never for advertising or newsletters.
- your **request for an interpreter (language)**, only if the teacher has provided for it in the round and you choose to give it. It is used solely to prepare the meeting. Within the booking service only the teacher and the administrators see it, and it is deleted there with the booking. The booking service names it in no e-mail. If the teacher arranges an interpreter, those arranging it learn the language and the appointment.
- the chosen **time slot**, the time of booking and the selected language.

If you provide an e-mail address, you receive confirmations with your appointment as a calendar file (.ics). On the page of your appointment you can also download the calendar file and have the confirmation sent to an e-mail address later (at most twice within 24 hours). That address is only used to send this e-mail and is not stored in the booking service. What you store in your own calendar is your own responsibility.

**When you visit the site:**

- **Abuse protection:** to prevent automated mass requests and guessing of codes, your IP address is shortened and then irreversibly hashed with a secret key, and stored together with the number of requests. So that these pages cannot send masses of e-mails to one address, your e-mail address is likewise stored only as such a hash, together with the number of e-mails sent to it. These entries are deleted after 24 hours at the latest.
- **Server logs:** the web host stores technical access data (including IP address, date and time, requested address, browser type) in log files. [ASK THE HOST FOR THE RETENTION PERIOD AND STATE IT HERE.]

There are no user accounts, no advertising, no tracking and no usage analytics. No external services are embedded (no external fonts, scripts, maps or videos). This site does not set cookies.

# 4. Legal basis

- **Booking, changing and cancelling:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR – processing is necessary to arrange the appointment you requested.
- **E-mail confirmation:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, at your request by voluntarily providing the address.
- **Abuse protection and server logs:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is the secure and reliable operation of the booking service and protection against spam, multiple and unauthorised bookings.
- **Summary e-mails to the teacher:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (carrying out the agreed appointment) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is the teacher's reliable appointment planning. The operator is not responsible for keeping them in the teacher's mailbox (see section 1).
- **Browser storage (“Remember my appointment”):** Section 25(1) TDDDG and Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (your consent by ticking the box), see section 7.

You do not need to give consent to book an appointment; the processing of your booking data is based on the legal grounds stated above. Consent only concerns the optional browser storage (“Remember my appointment”).

# 5. Retention and deletion

- **In the booking service** (database), your booking with name and e-mail address is **deleted automatically on the day after the appointment** (from midnight).
- If you cancel via your personal link, your details are deleted from the booking service **immediately**.
- If the teacher cancels your appointment or deletes a day or the booking round, the bookings concerned are deleted immediately. If you gave an e-mail address, you receive a cancellation by e-mail.
- Shortened and hashed IP addresses and hashed e-mail addresses (abuse protection) are deleted after 24 hours at the latest.
- **E-mails still to be sent** (e.g. the reminder the evening before, or a confirmation that could not be delivered at once): until it is sent, only a note of which e-mail is due for which booking is kept, without address or content; both are created from your booking when sending. The note is deleted once the e-mail is sent, after 48 hours at the latest, and together with your booking. The only exception is a cancellation by the teacher: your booking is already deleted then, so for this e-mail the address, language, name and appointment (without a link) are kept until it is sent and deleted afterwards, also after 48 hours at the latest.
- **Summary e-mails to the teacher** (see section 6) contain the number of appointments per day and a link to the appointment list, but no names and no e-mail addresses; time and name only if this has been allowed for the school. After sending, the booking service no longer stores them. How long the teacher keeps them in their mailbox follows the rules of their school (section 1). The operator recommends that teachers delete e-mails with names as soon as they no longer need them, at the latest at the end of the school year.
- If you would like these e-mails deleted, please contact the teacher or the school (Art. 17 GDPR). For your details in the booking service, a short message to the controller (section 1) is sufficient.
- Printed appointment lists made by the teacher are also subject to the rules of the school; the operator recommends destroying them after the appointment.

# 6. Recipients

Only the teacher of the booking round in which you booked and the persons whom the controller (section 1) has entrusted with administering this platform and bound to confidentiality (administrators) have access to the content of your details; other teachers on the platform cannot see them. If a booking round is assigned to another teacher (e.g. as a substitute), that teacher can see the existing bookings. They are not passed on to third parties for other purposes. Technically involved are:

- **Hosting:** [[HOSTER]]
- **E-mail:** via the web host's mail server (see above).
- **Translation of the teacher's texts:** the title and description of a booking round written by the teacher can be translated for display in several languages. Automatic translation takes place only in the teacher's browser on their own device, using the "Translator API" programming interface of Chrome or Edge with a locally stored translation model (not the browser's "translate page" feature). The texts are not sent to any translation service. On first use, the browser downloads the language model from its vendor (Google or Microsoft); no texts are transmitted in the process. **No data of parents** is translated, only these texts of the teacher.
- **Summaries to the teacher:** the teacher may receive an e-mail with the number of booked appointments, without e-mail addresses, on the evening before a meeting day and after the booking deadline; time and name only if this has been allowed for the school.

No data is transferred to countries outside the EU/EEA.

# 7. Browser storage

## Remember my appointment

Only if you tick “Remember my appointment on this device” when booking (it is not pre-ticked), your browser stores the link to your appointment, its description and its date in local storage (localStorage). When you open the page again, you see your appointments instead of the list of slots; if you book several (e.g. for siblings), each one is remembered. This data stays on your device and is not sent to the server.

You can withdraw this consent at any time using “Remove saved appointment from this device” on the booking page or by clearing the site data in your browser. It is removed automatically after a cancellation, and after the day of the appointment (the next time these pages are opened on your device).

## Appearance (light/dark)

If you choose “Light” or “Dark” at the bottom of the page, your browser stores this choice in local storage (localStorage) so that it is kept for your next visit. Only “light” or “dark” is stored; nothing is sent to the server. This storage is strictly necessary for the function you explicitly chose (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). Choosing “Automatic” deletes the entry; the page then follows your device setting again.

# 8. Security

The connection to this site is encrypted using TLS (HTTPS). Your personal link contains a long random key that is stored in the database only in irreversibly hashed form. Booking rounds may additionally be protected so that the code from the invitation is needed in addition to the link. Please do not share your personal link or the code with others.

# 9. Your rights

You have the following rights towards the controller regarding your personal data:

- right of **access** (Art. 15 GDPR)
- right to **rectification** (Art. 16 GDPR)
- right to **erasure** (Art. 17 GDPR)
- right to **restriction of processing** (Art. 18 GDPR)
- right to **data portability** (Art. 20 GDPR)
- right to **withdraw consent** with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR)

**Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR):** where processing is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation.

Please contact the controller named in section 1 or the teacher with whom you booked. For the meeting, notes, the summary e-mails in the teacher's mailbox or printouts (section 1), please contact the teacher or the school. Until the deadline the teacher has set for the booking round, you can also cancel your appointment yourself via your personal link, which deletes your details from the booking service immediately. Once the deadline has passed, your link shows a notice; please contact the teacher then.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), e.g. [COMPETENT STATE DATA PROTECTION AUTHORITY].

# 10. Obligation to provide data, no automated decisions

A name is required to book; without it a booking is not possible. Providing an e-mail address is optional. There is no automated decision-making, including profiling (Art. 22 GDPR).

This notice will be updated if the processing changes. The version published here applies.
