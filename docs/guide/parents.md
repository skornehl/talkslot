title: For parents

You received a link or a **round code** from your child's teacher. You do not need an account.

## Book an appointment

1. **Open the link**, or go to the start page of your school's booking site and enter the round code
   (for example `K7MP-3QXA`; dashes and upper or lower case do not matter).
2. **Choose a language** with the flags at the bottom of the page if you like: German, English, Polish,
   Russian, Ukrainian or Turkish (depending on the school).
3. **Pick a free time slot.** Only free slots are shown, grouped by day.
4. **Enter a name**, usually your child's first and last name. An **e-mail address is optional**: with it,
   you get a confirmation with your personal link and a calendar file.
5. Tick that you have read the **privacy notice** and book.

![Booking page on a phone]({{ '/assets/img/parent-round-mobile-dark.webp' | relative_url }}){: style="max-width: 320px"}

## Change or cancel

After booking you see your **personal link**. Keep it, for example as a bookmark: with it you can move the
appointment to another free slot or cancel it, until the deadline set by the teacher. If you entered an
e-mail address, the link is also in the confirmation mail, and the calendar entry updates itself when you
move the appointment.

![Confirmation page with the personal link]({{ '/assets/img/booking-confirmed.webp' | relative_url }})

## Good to know

- **Siblings:** book once per child, with the child's name. Some rounds allow only one appointment per
  person.
- **Remember on this device:** an optional checkbox saves the link to your appointment in your browser, so
  you see it when you open the round again. Nothing is sent to the server, and you can delete it any time.
- **Privacy:** the booking site sets no cookies and does not track you. Your details are deleted
  automatically the day after the appointment.
