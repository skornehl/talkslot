Public bodies, and so usually public schools, have to publish an **accessibility statement** for their websites
(EU Directive 2016/2102 and, in Germany, the accessibility law of the federal state). If your school runs TalkSlot
itself, this probably applies to your booking site. A privately run booking site is usually not covered.

This page is a template, **not legal advice**. Which law applies, the exact wording and the body parents can
turn to differ per federal state: please have it checked by the school authority or data protection officer.
As schools in Germany need it in German, the template is on the [German version of this page]({{ '/de/guide/accessibility-statement/' | relative_url }}).

## How to use it

1. Copy the template and replace everything in `[SQUARE BRACKETS]`.
2. Publish it on the school's website, or as a section at the end of the legal notice (*Texte → Impressum*):
   parents find it through the link in the footer of every page.
3. Check it once a year, and when you update TalkSlot.

## What the template says about TalkSlot

The list of limitations describes the software itself and is a self-assessment against WCAG 2.1 AA, not an
external audit. What you add yourself is your responsibility: descriptions of rounds, the legal texts and
linked PDF files. Translations of titles and descriptions are made by machine in the browser and may not be
perfect.

Current state of the software:

- operable with the keyboard, visible focus, a link to skip to the content;
- contrast of text and of controls according to WCAG AA, in the light and the dark display;
- labelled form fields, status messages for screen readers;
- the booking pages in six languages.

Known limitations:

- errors in forms are listed at the top of the form and are not yet shown at each field;
- the list of appointments of a round is a wide table that scrolls sideways on small screens (admin area);
- the admin area is available in German and English only.
