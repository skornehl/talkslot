Public bodies have to publish an **accessibility statement** for their websites (EU Directive 2016/2102 and, in
Germany, the accessibility law of the federal state). Whether this covers schools and a booking site for
parent-teacher meetings depends on the federal state: the Directive lets states exempt schools, except for
essential online administrative functions, and states have made different use of that. Who hosts the site makes
no difference. A booking site that is run privately, not by a public body, is usually not covered.

This page is a template, **not legal advice**. Which law applies, the exact wording and the body parents can
turn to differ per federal state: please clarify this with the school authority or the accessibility body of your
state. As schools in Germany need it in German, the template is on the
[German version of this page]({{ '/de/guide/accessibility-statement/' | relative_url }}).

## How to use it

1. Copy the template and replace everything in `[SQUARE BRACKETS]`.
2. Test your own booking site, with your own texts, and adjust the list of limitations.
3. Publish it as a clearly named page on the school's website and link to it from the legal notice
   (*Texte → Impressum*), so that parents can find it from every page of the booking site.
4. Check it once a year, and when you update TalkSlot.

## What the template says about TalkSlot

The list of limitations describes the software itself and comes from spot checks against WCAG 2.1 AA, not from a
complete audit. What you add yourself is your responsibility: descriptions of rounds, the legal texts and linked
PDF files.

Current state of the software:

- operable with the keyboard, visible focus, a link to skip to the content;
- contrast of text and of controls checked against WCAG AA, in the light and the dark display;
- labelled form fields, status messages for screen readers;
- the booking pages in six languages.

Known limitations:

- the booking form and the forms for rounds, days and bookings show errors at the field; the other forms of the admin area (accounts, templates apart from their breaks, profile, texts) list them at the top of the form and not yet at each field;
- the list of appointments of a round is a wide table that scrolls sideways on small screens (admin area);
- the admin area is available in German and English only.
